About Green Horizons

Committed to restoring urban ecosystems and empowering communities.

Our Story

Founded by local volunteers, Green Horizons began as a small neighborhood tree-planting effort and has grown into a citywide movement. Our work focuses on inclusion, education, and measurable environmental gains.

Mission

To engage people in restoring and protecting urban nature.

Vision

Healthy, green neighborhoods that support biodiversity and well-being.

Values

Equity, collaboration, science-led, community-driven action.
